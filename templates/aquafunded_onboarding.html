1. Understand the program

AquaFunded operates a proprietary-trading evaluation and simulated-account program. A traditional brokerage account can route real-money trades to a market. An AquaFunded simulated account uses virtual capital. Eligible cash rewards are subject to AquaFunded’s rules, terms and review.

OES may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through affiliate links.

Visit AquaFunded

AquaFunded is an independent provider. Participation can involve fees and loss of access after a rule breach. OES does not guarantee funding, profits, rewards or discounts.

2. Create your AquaFunded account

  1. Open AquaFunded and choose the account or registration option for the market you want.
  2. Follow the provider’s sign-up prompts and verify your email if prompted. If a verification message does not arrive, check spam and contact AquaFunded support.
  3. Keep your AquaFunded sign-in separate from your ChaseInGreen account and your assigned trading-platform credentials.

Instant and pay-after-pass

Instant programs skip the evaluation stage. Pay-after-pass plans use an evaluation with a separate activation fee after passing. Each has its own rules.

Instant Pro · Pay After Pass rules
Account size and platform
Compare the virtual starting balance and the platforms offered for that specific checkout. Availability can differ by program and region.
Profit targets and timing
Check the target for each stage, any minimum qualifying days and any inactivity deadline.
Daily and maximum drawdown
The daily limit governs losses over a defined day. The maximum limit applies to the account overall. Check which measures count open losses, fees and commissions, and when the daily calculation resets.
Static and trailing limits
A static floor stays anchored to a reference level. A trailing floor can rise with a new high and may use equity, including open positions. Use the provider’s exact calculation.
Consistency, payouts and restrictions
Check consistency, minimum days, the payout review and cycle, regional eligibility, and restrictions on trading methods, news, holding periods and instruments for the selected plan.

4. Review checkout

  1. Verify the exact program and variant, simulated account size and assigned platform.
  2. Review the total, currency, terms, add-ons and any activation or follow-on fee. If AquaFunded offers a promotional code, apply it on its site and check whether the total changes before paying.
  3. Complete payment through AquaFunded and keep its confirmation. OES does not collect payment details.

5. Read your AquaFunded dashboard

Open the account matching the order confirmation. Check whether it is active, inactive, under review or expired, and confirm the stage and remaining objectives. Compare balance with equity, which can change with open positions. Review daily and overall drawdown, progress toward targets, and any qualifying-day or reward conditions.

Use AquaFunded’s own dashboard or confirmation email to find assigned platform access and trading credentials. Keep those details private.

6. Open your assigned trading platform

Use the platform listed for your account rather than a general login link. If assigned MatchTrader, open the MatchTrader address supplied by AquaFunded and use the trading credentials it issued for that account. Check the selected account before trading. Do not enter that password on the OES website.

7. Set up ChaseInGreen

Install the public iPhone or iPad app, open it, and follow its sign-in and account creation prompts. Free covers essential market context and planning. Gold adds expanded AI market tools, alerts and analytics. The app supports planning and journaling and does not execute trades.

Use the released app’s account options only if it offers your provider and account type. A saved record or successful platform sign-in does not prove that synchronization works. Check the app’s actual status and use manual journaling if a connection is not offered. This guide does not confirm a released AquaFunded connection.

8. Troubleshoot problems

My credentials are rejected

Check whether the screen expects your AquaFunded sign-in or the separate credentials for the assigned trading platform. Verify the account and platform before trying again.

My account is missing, inactive or expired

Check your order confirmation, account identifier and stage in AquaFunded. Contact AquaFunded about activation, status, rule breaches or expiration.

My account is not syncing in ChaseInGreen

Check whether the public app offers that connection. If it does, confirm the account is active, check any last-sync time shown and note the error message. If data is missing or delayed, use the provider dashboard as the authoritative source.
