Your first session

  1. Install the public app. Use the App Store on an iPhone or iPad running version 18.5 or later.
  2. Open the app and follow the sign-in prompts. Start with Free to explore market context and planning before choosing a subscription.
  3. Try one workflow. Add a symbol to your watchlist, review its market context, then log a trade and review your decision.
  4. Connect an account only if the app offers your provider. Follow the instructions inside the app. This website does not collect broker credentials.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose or manage Gold?

Open Subscriptions in the app. Gold is $29.99 monthly or $299.99 annually in the U.S. Check the price Apple shows before confirming. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account subscriptions.

Can I trade through ChaseInGreen?

No. It provides market context, research, journaling and risk tools. It does not execute trades or guarantee investment or funding outcomes.

How does beta testing work?

Use TestFlight only if OES has authorized you to participate and sent an invitation. New customers should install from the App Store. For testing inquiries, contact OES.

What should I include in a support request?

Tell us the app version, your device model and what you were trying to do. Do not send passwords, broker credentials or account numbers.
