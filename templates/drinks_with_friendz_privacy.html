Drinks With Friendz

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Drinks With Friendz native iOS application (“Drinks With Friendz” or the “app”) accesses and uses information. The app is provided by Otis Execution Systems LLC (“OES,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Policy Sections

Information We Access How Information Is Used Location Information Google Maps and Google Places Third-Party Services and External Links Data Storage Analytics and Tracking Children’s Privacy Changes to This Privacy Policy Contact

01

Information We Access

With your permission, Drinks With Friendz accesses your device’s location while you use the app. The app uses that location to identify nearby bars, restaurants, cafés, nightlife, and other relevant places.

The app does not require an account or login. OES does not ask you to create a user profile through Drinks With Friendz.

02

How Information Is Used

Your location is used to center the map, find nearby places, calculate or present distance-related results, and support the app’s map and list experiences. Place information is used to show details such as names, photos, ratings, hours, addresses, and available contact or navigation actions.

03

Location Information

Drinks With Friendz requests location access only while the app is in use. It does not request or use background location access.

You can allow, deny, or change the app’s location permission at any time in iOS Settings. If location access is unavailable, location-dependent features may not work or may show limited results.

04

Google Maps and Google Places

Drinks With Friendz uses Google Maps Platform, including the Google Maps SDK for iOS and Google Places SDK for iOS, to provide maps and place data. This may include place names, photos, ratings, hours, addresses, geographic coordinates, and related place information.

Google and its services may process information in connection with these features under Google’s Privacy Policy and applicable terms. Google-required legal notices are also available inside the app.

Google reviews are not displayed in the current V3 app. Some Google place photos may also be omitted when the attribution required for those photos conflicts with the app’s photo presentation.

05

Third-Party Services and External Links

App actions such as Call, Website, Share, and Directions may open or interact with Apple, Google, a telephone carrier, a place’s website, or another third-party app or service. Those services operate independently and their own terms and privacy practices apply when you use them.

06

Data Storage

Drinks With Friendz does not have user accounts and does not operate a custom backend that stores user profiles, location histories, or other user data. The app and third-party SDKs may temporarily process or cache information as needed to provide maps, place results, and photos.

07

Analytics and Tracking

Drinks With Friendz does not currently use OES-owned analytics or advertising tracking. OES does not sell users’ personal information. Third-party services used by the app may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

08

Children’s Privacy

Drinks With Friendz is not directed to children under 13, and OES does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted at this URL with its effective date.
